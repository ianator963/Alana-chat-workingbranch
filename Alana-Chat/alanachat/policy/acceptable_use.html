ALANA Community Platform
Acceptable Use Summary

The ALANA Community Platform is intended to support respectful communication and collaboration among members of the Rochester Institute of Technology community.

By participating on this platform, users agree to follow these basic expectations:

  • Communicate respectfully with other members of the RIT community.
  • Follow all applicable RIT policies and student conduct guidelines.
  • Do not post harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive content.
  • Do not share confidential or sensitive information.
  • Use the platform only for relevant community discussion and collaboration.

Platform administrators may remove content or restrict access if these expectations are not followed.

For complete details, please review the full ALANA Community Use Policy.
