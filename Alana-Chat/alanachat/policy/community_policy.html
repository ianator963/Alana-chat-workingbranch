ALANA Community Platform
Universal Community Use Policy

1. Purpose

The ALANA Community Platform exists to support communication, collaboration, and resource sharing among members of the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) community involved with ALANA-affiliated student organizations. This platform provides a centralized environment for discussion, information exchange, and coordination between organizations and members of the campus community.

This document establishes baseline expectations for participation on the platform. It does not replace the policies of individual student organizations or the policies of Rochester Institute of Technology, but instead provides a shared framework for respectful and responsible participation.

2. Applicability

This policy applies to all individuals who access or participate in the ALANA Community Platform. Access to the platform is restricted to authenticated members of the RIT community.

All users are expected to comply with applicable institutional policies including:

  • RIT Student Code of Conduct
  • RIT Information Technology policies
  • RIT Student Government and CampusGroups organization guidelines
  • Policies established by individual student organizations

Where conflicts occur, official RIT policies take precedence.

3. Eligibility and Access

Access to the platform is restricted to authenticated users through RIT institutional login systems.

Eligible users may include:

  • Currently enrolled RIT students
  • RIT faculty and staff
  • Other authorized members of the RIT community

Access may be restricted or revoked if a user violates this policy or applicable RIT policies.

4. Expected Conduct

Users are expected to participate in a manner consistent with the values of the RIT community and standards of respectful communication.

Appropriate use includes:

  • Constructive discussion and collaboration
  • Sharing information relevant to student organizations and campus initiatives
  • Respectful communication with members of the RIT community

Users must not:

  • Harass, threaten, or intimidate other participants
  • Post discriminatory, hateful, or abusive content
  • Share confidential or sensitive information without authorization
  • Use the platform for spam, solicitation, or unrelated commercial activity
  • Attempt to disrupt or compromise the technical operation of the platform

5. Organizational Autonomy

Individual student organizations retain full authority over their internal policies, membership expectations, and leadership structures. Participation in this platform does not override or replace any policies maintained by individual clubs or organizations.

6. Content Responsibility

Users are responsible for the content they post or share on the platform. Content should reflect the values of the RIT community and maintain a respectful and professional tone.

Platform administrators reserve the right to remove content that violates this policy or applicable RIT policies, or that creates an unsafe or disruptive environment.

7. Privacy and Data Use

The platform uses RIT authentication systems to verify user identity. Information obtained through authentication is used only to provide access and maintain accountability within the platform.

Users should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information within public discussions.

8. Moderation and Enforcement

While the platform is designed to support open communication, administrators may intervene when necessary to maintain compliance with institutional standards and community expectations.

Administrative actions may include:

  • Content removal
  • Temporary or permanent suspension of access
  • Referral to appropriate RIT administrative offices

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in platform functionality, institutional policy, or community needs. Users are encouraged to review this document periodically.

10. Acknowledgment

By accessing or using the ALANA Community Platform, users acknowledge that they have read and agree to comply with this policy and with applicable Rochester Institute of Technology policies governing student conduct and use of university systems.
