ALANA Community Platform
Privacy Notice

1. Purpose

This Privacy Notice explains how the ALANA Community Platform collects, uses, and protects information associated with user access to the platform. The platform is intended for members of the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) community and relies on institutional authentication systems to verify user identity.

2. Authentication

Access to the platform is provided through Rochester Institute of Technology’s institutional authentication system. Users authenticate using their RIT credentials through the university’s secure login services.

The platform does not collect or store user passwords. Authentication is handled entirely by RIT's identity systems.

3. Information Collected

When users authenticate through RIT, the platform may receive limited identity attributes from the university's authentication service. These may include:

  • RIT username or user identifier
  • RIT email address
  • Name associated with the RIT account
  • Affiliation within the RIT community (such as student or employee)

These attributes are used solely for the purpose of identifying users within the platform and maintaining accountability for posted content.

4. Use of Information

Information obtained through authentication may be used to:

  • Verify eligibility to access the platform
  • Associate posts and comments with authenticated users
  • Maintain a safe and accountable community environment
  • Enforce the platform's community policy and RIT policies

5. Data Storage

User identity attributes may be temporarily stored within session data while the user is actively logged into the platform. These sessions are used only to maintain authenticated access during use of the platform.

The platform does not intentionally collect or store unnecessary personal data beyond what is required to support authentication and platform functionality.

6. User Responsibility

Users should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information within public discussions on the platform. Content posted by users may be visible to other authenticated members of the RIT community.

7. Institutional Policies

Use of this platform is subject to applicable Rochester Institute of Technology policies governing the use of university systems, technology resources, and student conduct.

8. Changes to This Notice

This Privacy Notice may be updated periodically to reflect changes in platform functionality or institutional requirements. Users are encouraged to review the notice periodically.
